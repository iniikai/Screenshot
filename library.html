What you can do

  • Capture three ways — visible tab, the whole scrolling page, or drag to select a region.
  • Keyboard shortcuts for all three, remappable to whatever you like.
  • Collect everything in one library instead of scattering files in Downloads.
  • Search across titles, notes, URLs and tags. Filter by site.
  • Rename and add notes, one at a time or a whole batch at once.
  • Tag screenshots to group them however you think.
  • Annotate — pen, arrows, boxes, and blur to hide anything private.
  • Compare any two side by side.
  • Duplicate detection flags near-identical captures.
  • Bulk select, then rename, download or delete in one go.
  • Export the whole library as a single zip.
  • Drag a screenshot straight out into Slack, Figma or an email.
  • Pick your format — PNG, WebP or JPEG, to trade quality against space.
  • Undo deletes for six seconds. Auto-delete old ones if you want.
  • Dark mode, following your system.
  • Stays on your device — no account, no cloud, no tracking, no network requests.
